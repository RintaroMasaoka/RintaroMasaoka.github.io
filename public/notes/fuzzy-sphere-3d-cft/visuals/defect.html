A line defect on the cylinder

Punctured flat space
Cylinder
Two rays become two worldlines
Two sites per time slice

The two rays of the through-origin line meet every sphere at N,S. They map to two time-directed worldlines, intersecting each time slice at two sites. The sphere slices are the same as in the bulk cylinder map. Left circles are sphere sections; right ellipses represent whole S2 slices with one angular direction suppressed.
