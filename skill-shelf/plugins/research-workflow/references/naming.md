# Naming Contract

Apply this contract only when reusing a short expression as a heading, dispatch handle, child name, map entry, or repeated label. Do not turn all local prose into a glossary.

For a reusable name, specify:

- expansion: an explanation for a first-time reader
- grounding: source, domain, project-definition, project-observation, user-decision, mixed, or unknown
- stability: nonce, provisional, active, deprecated, or retired
- carry_scope: local, node, subtree, project, or paper
- claim_permission: name-only, definition-only, evidence-linked, or claim-linked
- merge_boundary: nearby concepts that may or may not be treated as equivalent
- route: inline, concepts, conventions, source, findings/checks, or plain prose

Working documents may record Naming decisions in this single-line format:

    - {name}: expansion: {...}. Grounding: {...}. Stability: {...}. Carry scope: {...}. Claim permission: {...}. Merge boundary: {...}. Proposed route: {...}.

concepts/{term}.md helps readers understand a term; it does not prove a claim. Route notation to conventions, source-native terms to source records, and project claims to findings/checks. In durable prose, replace administrative labels with definitions, links, sources, verified claims, or plain prose.
