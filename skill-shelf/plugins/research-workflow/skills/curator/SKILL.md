# Legacy compatibility

There is no separate curator authority. On an explicit legacy request, read [core](../../references/core.md) and [verification](../../references/verification.md), and perform the requested authorized update under those contracts. The research lead owns scientific conclusions and their records. Scripts perform deterministic maintenance; the lower model tier handles remaining routine interpretation. Do not spawn a curator, require promotion approval, duplicate reviews, or generate an extraction manifest. Preserve existing evidence and mark changed/retracted claims and affected dependents. Continue the actual requested research task through $research-workflow:auto-research when needed; do not recursively dispatch this compatibility entrypoint.
