# Research project

Replace this paragraph with the project's research question, scientific scope, source locations, computational environment, and any restrictions on writing or publication. State physical assumptions and notation explicitly.

## Research workflow

Use `research-workflow:auto-research` for autonomous research cycles. Its bounded supporting roles are `research-workflow:research-planner`, `research-workflow:direction-challenger`, `research-workflow:researcher`, `research-workflow:critic`, `research-workflow:curator`, and `research-workflow:guide-writer`. Do not dispatch these roles by bare local name.

Keep project evidence and results under `research/`. The project owner decides research direction and publication; the workflow records uncertainty and independent-review debt rather than treating an agent verdict as proof.
