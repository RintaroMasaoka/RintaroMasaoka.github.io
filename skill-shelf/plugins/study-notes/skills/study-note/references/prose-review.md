# Prose review for a study note

Localized from `academic-writing:prose-review` and its sentence-clarity, expository-progression, reader-state, and working-memory perspectives. Use for a requested prose edit or review. Select only the concerns implicated by the request; a narrow wording change does not call for an exhaustive scientific or style audit.

## Document-level pass

Recover the note's purpose, audience, source authority, and the current published manuscript. Map each chapter or section to the question it takes up, the result it establishes, and the later step that uses that result. Inspect transitions from the reader's position. A roadmap or summary earns its place when it helps locate or use a dependency; repeated promises, takeaways, and descriptions of what the author will do can displace the actual relation.

For a suspicious passage, reconstruct the smallest subject-level chain: setup → result or constraint → open choice or required relation → next calculation or claim. Keep an explanation that supplies an object, premise, or usable navigation. Remove or replace an editorial announcement that merely says an explanation is coming. Do not turn the correction into reader-facing language about the note's own organization or an earlier draft. Questions and contrasts should arise from a real result, ambiguity, failed step, or relevant audience difficulty, not from a template.

## Paragraph and sentence pass

At first use, check technical terms, symbols, and ordinary role words such as “input,” “correction,” “remainder,” or “choice.” Identify the object and operation that give each its role. A glossary entry does not repair a missing relation. Check that terms retain the same referent across prose, formulas, figures, and chapters.

For each burdensome sentence, identify its subject, assertion, conditions, and link to surrounding reasoning before shortening it. Repair delayed predicates, nested conditions, ambiguous attachment, and abrupt jumps while keeping all necessary assumptions and intermediate reasoning. Direct verbs often help, but passive voice and long precise sentences can be appropriate. Repetition is useful when it connects representations or restores an input at a later inference; remove only repetition that performs no such work.

Use the [working-memory review](working-memory-review.md) when the reader must retain notation mappings, suspended questions, or separated premises. Difficulty, length, or symbol count alone does not establish overload. A heading or paragraph is not defective merely because it announces a topic; ask what reading or inference it enables.

## Output and verification

For an authorized edit, leave clean prose in the manuscript; keep diagnosis, rejected alternatives, and repair history outside it. For review-only work, report exact spans, consequences, and concrete repairs. Re-read every changed sentence in its paragraph and the relevant chapter transition. Preserve equations, claims, modality, quantifiers, citations, source terminology, and reference integrity. If a scientific ambiguity cannot be resolved from checked evidence, report it instead of silently choosing a smoother claim. After a substantive change, test the next use using only the visible text available up to that point.
