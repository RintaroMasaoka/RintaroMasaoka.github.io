# Sentence clarity

Use for an identified burden in scientific prose or as a selected dimension of a comprehensive writing review. Establish passage, genre, audience, language, and edit/review scope. Ambiguity about scope is not a request to run every check over the entire manuscript.

## Inspect meaning before compression

Identify the sentence's subject, assertion, conditions, and connection to surrounding reasoning. Remove a phrase only when those functions remain recoverable. A repeated premise or intermediate step may be necessary for teaching. Do not replace qualified associations with causal verbs, remove meaningful qualifications, or simplify a technical expression because its words resemble everyday redundancy.

## Agency and verbs

Make agency explicit when it changes interpretation. Prefer a direct verb when it preserves the exact relation; a nominal expression may denote a legitimate mathematical object. Keep passive voice when the actor is irrelevant or the object is the appropriate topic. Do not invent an actor or default to authorial “we” without evidence.

## Sentence architecture

Locate the reading operation made difficult by nested qualifications, delayed predicates, ambiguous attachment, or abrupt transitions. Restructure so the intended reader can recover the relation while retaining required conditions. Use punctuation and varied length where they help that operation; word distances and rhythmic uniformity are diagnostic cues, not rejection thresholds. A long precise sentence can remain unchanged.

## Terms and abbreviations

Keep one referent's technical identity stable across prose, equations, captions, and tables. Preserve an explicitly introduced alternative notation or a justified distinction. Judge abbreviation familiarity against the actual audience, not a universal acronym list. Independent reading surfaces may require local introductions; apply the artifact and venue requirements. Use the reader-vocabulary perspective for first-use availability and the term-status perspective for whether an expression earns a name.

## Consistency and evidence

Check visible repeated numbers or claims for discrepancies when relevant to the requested writing review. Report a discrepancy with exact locations rather than deciding scientific truth from prose alone. Follow a specialized source or claim audit when verification is requested; a wording review is not evidence that all numbers, sources, or equations were audited.

## Output and verification

For review, identify each material defect, the affected span, a concrete revision, and what it preserves or improves. Rank by its effect on interpretation rather than imposing a fixed issue count. For editing, return clean prose and keep findings outside the artifact. For an interactive request, work at the requested pace. Re-read each changed sentence in its paragraph and check claim strength, reader prerequisites, term identity, and reference integrity. Preserve effective prose even when another style is possible.
