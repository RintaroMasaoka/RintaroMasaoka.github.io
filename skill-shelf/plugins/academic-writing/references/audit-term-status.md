# Audit Term Status

Use this task when scholarly prose turns a task-local description into a
compact compound or nominal label whose status and internal relations are not
available to the reader. The failure is not that the expression is long or
that a particular kind of writer produced it. It is that local familiarity is
mistaken for lexical establishment, or naming suppresses information still
needed to understand the claim.

## Inclusion test

Revise an expression only when all of the following hold:

1. it concatenates nouns, modifiers, or technical labels into a compact name;
2. the expression is not demonstrably established in the relevant discipline
   or defined earlier for a real reuse purpose; and
3. understanding the surrounding claim requires the reader to reconstruct a
   relation, operation, actor, object, condition, direction, or comparison that
   the name suppresses.

Do not flag a standard technical compound merely because its internal meaning
is specialized. Do not flag a locally defined name when later reuse makes the
name earn its cost and the defining relation is available before use.

## Repair

1. Recover the uncompressed proposition: identify what acts or varies, what it
   acts on or is compared with, and under which condition.
2. Restore the smallest missing relation in ordinary syntax. Prefer a verb,
   relative clause, particle relation, or explicit modifier over another coined
   nominal label.
3. Keep an established head term when only its task-local modifiers are
   overpacked.
4. If the expression is used only once, state its content directly instead of
   naming it. If it recurs and a short name materially helps, define the name
   after stating the content and then use it consistently.
5. Re-read the paragraph as a first-time reader. The revision passes only if
   the reader no longer needs task history to recover the suppressed relation.

## Evidence and ambiguity

Use the manuscript's terminology record, nearby definitions, representative
literature supplied for the task, and the target venue's established usage to
decide whether a compound is conventional. Frequency inside the current AI
session is not evidence of establishment.

If two decompressions encode different scientific relations, do not choose by
fluency. In review mode, state the ambiguity and the alternatives. In edit
mode, preserve the claim and mark the unresolved span for author input rather
than inventing a relation.

## Output

- **Edit:** clean revised prose, preserving unaffected text and all technical
  content.
- **Review:** exact expression, the hidden relation that must be recovered,
  whether the term appears established, and a concrete revision or ambiguity
  note.

Do not leave diagnostic labels such as “ad hoc,” “overcompressed,” or
“decompressed” in the scholarly artifact.
