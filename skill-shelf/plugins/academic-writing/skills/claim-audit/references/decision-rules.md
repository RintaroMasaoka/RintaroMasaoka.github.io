# Decision rules sent to audit workers

Read this when preparing or evaluating worker packets. Copy the common rule and
only the assigned dimension into each packet. Preserve the decision question,
required witness and boundary case; replacing them with a label such as "check
reader state" loses the contract. Shared batches may carry these rules once.
The required witness can be a few sentences in the ledger's `evidence` field;
no new schema or long report is required.

## Common rule

Judge one exact assertion or expository contribution under the supplied reader
contract and source scope. Return pass, gap, unknown or split, with a locator
and the witness below. If a witness needs several unresolved judgments, split
those questions and retain a separate join. Do not write replacement prose.
Do not assume a defect exists. Distinguish an observed missing explanation from
uncertainty about the proposition's truth. A gap requires a specific failed
relation; uncertainty about unsupplied context is unknown.

## Warrant

Question: What licensed premises and operation establish this exact conclusion?

Witness: State the premise, its authority, the inference and the conclusion
under its conditions. Test whether the cited "evidence" merely restates the
target, including in a later formula, supplement or paraphrase. If it does,
it cannot establish that target. For an identification, matching dimensions,
labels or symmetries establishes only what those matches entail; identify the
construction or theorem that licenses the asserted map and its interpretation.

Distinguish material supplied for inspection from premises authorized for use.
A formula inside the inspected source is a claim to audit, not an authorized
assumption just because the packet includes it. When a witness uses an asserted
correspondence or model relation to establish the learning target, suspend the
warrant verdict and dispatch the premise-license check below.

Classify the target's responsibility before demanding proof. A definition can
stipulate notation; an explicit assumption can support a conditional calculation;
an external theorem may be licensed by an exact statement and checked hypotheses.
Check these within their scope, without requiring all foundations to be derived.
The passage's promised explanation cannot be made an assumed premise merely to
pass it. Verification of downstream algebra does not verify its starting model.

## Availability

Question: Can this reader perform the next required operation here?

Witness: State the actual operation, the relationship it uses, and where that
relationship is usable in the reader contract or preceding text. Distinguish
"known", "introduced but not yet usable", and "not supplied". A definition may
settle a symbol without establishing a correspondence involving it. Later
material may support warrant while being unavailable on the present reading path.

Name the specific dependency or retrieval burden, not a list of unfamiliar
terms. Licensed background does not need re-teaching solely because it is used.
If uncertainty about the audience would reverse the decision, name that premise
and return unknown rather than inventing a beginner/expert profile.

## Role

Question: What does this span enable for this reader at this position?

Witness: State what the reader can already do, the specific advance or retrieval
the span supplies, and the next use of that result. Topic relevance alone does
not establish this contribution. If the content is already known, identify the
new application, comparison, needed recall or other concrete contribution that
warrants its presence here. With no such evidence, do not certify necessity.

Compare the local reading with the explanatory span omitted while retaining
necessary definitions and references. Name what understanding or needed recall
would be lost; do not infer that all removable wording should be deleted. Useful
repetition and preparatory learning can pass without adding a new fact. Preparation
needs a purpose this reader can recognize now, not just a later technical use.
For a mixed paragraph, separate the useful connection from any further retelling;
one useful sentence does not license the whole paragraph.

## Integration check

Before accepting a verdict, compare its witnesses across dimensions. If
availability says "already known" while role says "teaches this relation", require
a specified additional contribution rather than accepting the two explanations
unchanged. If warrant assumes a relationship that availability identifies as the
learning target, distinguish a conditional calculation from establishing that
relationship. Apply the same reader contract to every requested extra explanation.
Accept or reject individual findings; a valid bottleneck does not validate all
suggestions in its batch. Record disagreements and reopen only the affected task.

## Premise-license check (conditional task)

Use when a proposed warrant depends on a relation stated in the inspected source
whose license has not been established. Give a worker just the target, the exact
candidate premise and its preceding justification, and the audience/task contract.
Ask one question: **May this relation be used as an independent premise for this
target?** Its witness must classify the relation as contracted background,
explicitly assumed for this conditional task, independently established (identify
the inference), or still an assertion being inspected. The last category cannot
license itself. Compare semantic content, not notation or source location.

Record this check as a child of the affected warrant, alongside its conditional
derivation and a distinct join, or reuse its existing prerequisite claim check.
Do not leave it as an unreferenced task in the ledger.

If the relation is still under inspection, create its own warrant task and
withhold support for downstream conclusions until it is resolved. A downstream
calculation may pass conditionally if its original scope is preserved, but that
pass must not certify the correspondence or model from which it starts. A known
source formula may be a legitimate external input only when the task contract
licenses that use and its statement and hypotheses are checked.

Do not run this extra task on every elementary definition. Trigger it when the
witness imports an unlicensed model/identification or cannot distinguish its
premise from the target. Passing unrelated algebra or low-energy estimates does
not resolve this premise-license obligation.
